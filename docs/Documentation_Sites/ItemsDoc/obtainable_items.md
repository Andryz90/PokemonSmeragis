# Obtainable Items

Baseline document for item-balance tracking.

## Scope
- Progression covered: **up to the Norman split**.
- Tracked items: **Heart Scale** and **Rare Candy**.
- Choice entries indicate mutually exclusive outcomes.

## Splits

### Roxanne Badge
- Petalburg City: one choice between **Heart Scale** and **Rare Candy**.
- Route 104: **Heart Scale**.
- Rustboro: **Rare Candy**.

### Brawly Badge
- Rustboro Rival reward: **Heart Scale** or **Rare Candy**, depending on the first choice.
- Route 106 Hidden Item: **Heart Scale**.
- Route 106 Hidden Item: **Rare Candy**.
- Granite Cave: **Heart Scale**.
- Route 109 Hidden Item: **Rare Candy**.

### Wattson Badge
- Trick House reward: **Rare Candy**.
- Route 103: **Heart Scale**.
- Route 110: **Heart Scale**.
- Route 118: **Heart Scale**.
- Route 117: **Rare Candy**.
- Route 116: **Heart Scale**.
- Route 111: **Rare Candy**.
- Dune Cave: one choice between **Heart Scale** and **Ability Patch**.

### Mt. Chimney Split
- Route 112: one choice between **Heart Scale** and **Ability Patch**.
- Route 114: **Heart Scale**.

### Flannery Badge
- Meteor Falls 1F: **Rare Candy**.
- Route 115: **Heart Scale**.
- Route 115: **Rare Candy**.
- Mt. Chimney: one choice between **Rare Candy** and **Heart Scale**.
- Jagged Pass: **Rare Candy**.

### Route 111 Desert
- **Rare Candy**.

### Norman Split
- Cycling Road: one choice between **Heart Scale** and **Rare Candy**.

## Declared Totals
- **Heart Scales up to Norman:** 14 (10 guaranteed, excluding choice outcomes)
- **Rare Candies up to Norman:** 13 (10 guaranteed, excluding choice outcomes)
